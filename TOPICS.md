# Topic bank — POV first-timer rules, daily-life Japan (from the 5 theme areas)

Priority: retail & food first (proven by the reference account), then transit, events, conversation.

## Retail
1. konbini_tray — Cash goes on the tray, not in the clerk's hand. (Ep.1)
2. konbini_bag — They ask "bag?" — a bag costs 3 yen; and warm/cold items get separate bags.
3. konbini_points — "ポイントカードはお持ちですか？" comes before you pay.
4. supermarket_basket — In a supermarket, put your basket on the stand, don't hand it over.

## Food
5. ramen_ticket — Buy your ticket first, then sit. (proven format; our 3D take)
6. sushi_soy — Don't drown the nigiri in soy sauce; dip the fish side, lightly.
7. izakaya_otoshi — Everyone pays for a dish nobody ordered: the otoshi is the table charge.
8. buffet_tray — At a buffet, take a tray first; new plate for each round.
9. water_free — Water and tea are free and often self-serve; nobody will bring a bill for them.

## Transit
10. train_silence — Phone on silent (manner mode); no calls on the train.
11. train_line — Stand in the marked lines on the platform; let everyone off first.
12. suica_gate — Tap, don't insert; and charge before the gate, not at it.
13. bus_pay — On many city buses you pay when you get OFF, flat fare at the front.

## Conversation
14. sumimasen — To call staff, say sumimasen loudly — it's not rude, it's the system.
15. oshibori — The wet towel is for your hands, not your face.

## Events
16. matsuri_garbage — At festivals there are almost no trash cans; carry your garbage home.
17. hatsumode_coins — At the shrine: bow twice, clap twice, pray, bow once.
